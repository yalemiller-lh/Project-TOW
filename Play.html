    Battlefield rules

    Play against a bot (the default) to command the red army against a bot, or choose Two players in the top bar to share this screen. Red commands three Chaos Dwarf Warrior regiments, one Blunderbuss Decimator regiment, and a Deathshrieker Rocket Launcher with three crew. Before deployment, choose either green Orc Mobs with a warbow regiment or blue Empire State Troops with a crossbow regiment and two Great Cannons for the opposing army. Each regiment has 20 individual models in five files and four ranks. The front rank contains a Musician (M), Standard Bearer (S), and Champion (C), with the standard in the centre.

    How to play

    Against a bot, you deploy first and the bot then deploys the opposing Orc or Empire army in response to your line and commands it. It pauses for your charge reactions, dispels and combat aftermath choices. You can switch back to Two players at any time.

    1. Choose the opposing army. Place all eight blocks, the launcher, and both Empire cannons (when selected) in their coloured 12″ deployment zones, or use Quick deploy.
    2. Advance the marker through Strategy, Movement, Shooting and Combat. During Movement, declare charges when available, roll charge moves, then move remaining regiments. A charge has one measured approach wheel and a free wheel to align after contact; it can strike the front, flank or rear. Click a unit on the flock table for its orders and inch bar.
    3. During Shooting, click a missile regiment to see its front arc, the 50% close-range line, and highlighted targets. Select a target on the board; regiments in combat cannot be targeted. The popup shows hit modifiers, firing models and wound rolls. Until your army acts in Shooting, Back to Movement reopens Remaining Moves so earlier moves can be undone. When charged, click the marked defender to choose Hold or Flee; an eligible missile defender may Stand & Shoot. Flee pivots away and moves 2D6″ before the charge roll.
    4. Click the Deathshrieker in the Chaos Dwarf Shooting phase. Choose Demolition Rockets (3″ blast) or Infernal Incendiaries (5″ blast), choose a target, then roll Artillery and Scatter dice. The template shows aim and impact on the table; Misfires use the Black Powder table.
    5. During the Empire Shooting phase, click a Great Cannon. Choose a 60″ cannonball with an aim point up to 10″ short of an enemy, or 12″ grapeshot. Roll Artillery dice and resolve any Misfire. Cannon, rocket, and missile hits show a brief impact effect on the battlefield.
    6. In Combat, click either engaged unit. Show Initiative, fight each Initiative tier in order, compare results and roll the loser’s Break test. Choose Shieldwall when available, move the loser, then choose the winner’s follow up/pursuit or restraint. Pursuers move after catching fleeing units; those that leave the board return near their exit point in their next Movement phase. Destroying the enemy before the Break test allows an overrun: 2D6 straight forward with no pivot, or a Leadership test to restrain and reform (failure forces the overrun). An overrun that meets a fresh enemy wheels to align and counts as charging; one that contacts nothing may test Leadership to reform. An overrun off the battlefield returns just inside that edge in the unit's next Movement phase. Empty phases and subphases skip automatically. A broken unit can attempt Rally in its next Strategy phase.

    Chaos Dwarfs carry hand weapons, heavy armour and shields. Orcs carry hand weapons and light armour. State Troops carry hand weapons, light armour and the optional shield. No command upgrades are present.

    Shooting weapons

    Deathshrieker: 12–48″, 50 × 75 mm base with three 25 mm crew. Demolition Rockets: 3″ blast, S3 (S6 and AP −3 under the central hole). Infernal Incendiaries: 5″ blast, S3 and Flaming Attacks. Both use Bombardment and the Black Powder Misfire table.

    Empire Great Cannon: 60″ cannonball, S10/AP −3, strike and bounce Artillery dice; or 12″ grapeshot, S4/AP −1, one Artillery die of automatic hits. Two 50 × 75 mm cannons each have three crew. They are stationary and cannot be shot at, but enemy regiments can charge them. Each war machine pivots to face its target before shooting (this is not a move), and no unit can move through one.

    Blunderbusses: 12″, S3, AP −1, D3 shots, Volley Fire; ignore long-range and Stand & Shoot hit penalties. Crossbows: 30″, S4. Orc warbows: 24″, S3. A normal move gives −1 to hit; marched or fleeing units cannot shoot or Stand & Shoot. Hits show a brief impact flash on the target. Beyond half range gives −1 unless ignored by the weapon. Stand & Shoot gives −1 with no long-range penalty. Each firing model checks its own front arc, range and clear line of sight.

    Measurements and combat

    The table is 72″ × 48″. All unit bases, rotations, movement and contact are measured in tabletop inches. Chaos Dwarfs move 3″ or march 6″; Orcs and State Troops move 4″ or march 8″. A charge rolls 2D6, keeps the higher die and adds Movement. Orc Warband automatically rerolls a failed charge range. Orc Mobs that can charge must test Impetuous in their Declare Charges step; a failed test forces a charge.

    Attacks use the opposing Weapon Skill and Toughness. Hand weapon and shield units use Parry. Orcs gain Furious Charge after charging at least 3″, and Choppas reroll natural 1s to wound and improve Armour Piercing on a charge. Warband adds the Orc rank bonus to Leadership where allowed. Combat scores include wounds, ranks, Close Order, Massed Infantry, flank or rear, and +1 for a living standard. Most champions have two Attacks; the State Missile Troop Sergeant has BS4 and one Attack. A sole musician wins an otherwise tied combat and improves rally and nearby march Leadership tests by +1. The loser makes a Break test. An applicable Chaos Dwarf Shieldwall is offered as the loser’s choice.

    War machines in combat: the Deathshrieker and Great Cannons can be charged like any unit and may only Hold. The machine and its crew are one model; the crew bases are tokens. In combat it uses the crew profile (Chaos Dwarf crew WS3 S3 T4 I2 Ld9; Empire crew WS3 S3 T3 I3 Ld7), makes one Attack per surviving crew token, has no armour save, and each wound removes a crew token. It has no flanks or rear. It never moves: if it breaks, the crew abandon it and it is destroyed; otherwise it stays in the fight. A winning war machine cannot pursue or overrun, and an engaged one cannot fire.

    Battle Magic

    The Chaos Dwarf Daemonsmith and Empire Battlemage are Level 2 wizards on 25 mm bases with two Wounds. Both randomly generate two Battle Magic spells at battle start. In the first Strategy phase, exchange one for Hammerhand; the Daemonsmith may choose a Lore of Hashut signature instead. Click a wizard for spells and legal targets. Spells use the Strategy, Remaining Moves, Shooting and Combat phases. The opponent can attempt a Wizardly or Fated dispel where allowed. Double 1 miscasts and double 6 cannot be dispelled. Pillar of Fire remains on the field, scatters each turn and may be dispelled. The Daemonsmith may re-roll an Artillery or Scatter die for a nearby Deathshrieker once per turn. Battle Magic for the Daemonsmith is a scenario house rule: its published lore list excludes Battle Magic.

    Prototype limits

    One-on-one combats only. The charge planner searches in 5-degree steps and may miss some legal tabletop approaches. The loser moves before the winner chooses in this prototype; published Old World rules place the winner’s choice first. Defenders choose Hold, Flee, or Stand & Shoot when eligible. No other terrain, character joining, challenges or directed attacks against champions, missile panic, victory conditions, multiple chargers, charge redirection or full compulsory fleeing movement. Fleeing units pass through other units: friends passed through test Panic (and may flee in turn), models that crossed an enemy take a Peril test (1–3 loses a wound), and the flee continues until the unit is 1″ clear. Casualties are removed from one end of the rear rank (command models last); losses suffered before a unit strikes still reduce its fighting ranks' attacks that phase. Blocks keep their 5 × 4 footprint. The game resets on reload. Renegades 2.0 is a community Chaos Dwarf army list, separate from Games Workshop's official legacy list.
