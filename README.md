# Warhammer: The Old World — Battlefield

Double-click **Play.html** to play offline. Two players share one screen. Red has three Chaos Dwarf Warrior regiments and one Blunderbuss Decimator regiment, each of 20 models, a Level 2 Daemonsmith Sorcerer, plus a Deathshrieker Rocket Launcher and three crew. Before deployment, choose green Orc Mobs (including one with warbows) or blue Empire State Troops (including one State Missile Troop regiment with crossbows, two Great Cannons, and a Level 2 Battlemage) for the opposing army. Every infantry regiment includes a musician (M), standard bearer (S), and champion (C) in the centre of its front rank; they replace three of the 20 ordinary models. Quick deploy, or place each regiment, wizard, and war machine in its 12-inch deployment zone. The top marker follows Strategy → Movement → Shooting → Combat.

Choose **Play against AI** in the top bar for a solo game as the Chaos Dwarfs. The computer deploys and controls the opposing Orc or Empire army, tests Impetuous and Rally, charges, moves, shoots, fires cannons, and resolves combat. It automatically chooses reactions when you charge it. When it charges or a combat result calls for your decision, the game pauses for your charge reaction, Shieldwall choice, or pursuit choice. The AI uses simple distance and legal-action priorities; you can switch back to **Two players** at any time. Quick deploy remains available, or you can deploy the Chaos Dwarfs yourself after the AI has placed its army.

Both wizards are independent, unmounted 25 mm characters with two Wounds. At battle start, each randomly knows two different Battle Magic spells. On their first Strategy phase, you may exchange one of those for Hammerhand; the Daemonsmith may instead choose one of the three Lore of Hashut signatures. Click a wizard to see the spells and legal targets. Cast Enchantments and Hexes in Strategy, Arcane Urgency during Remaining Moves, Magic Missiles and Pillar of Fire in Shooting, and Assailments in Combat. Casting and Wizardly dispels use 2D6 +1 at Level 2; a Fated Dispel uses unmodified 2D6 when no Wizardly dispel is available. Double 1 miscasts; double 6 is irresistible. The opponent response selector lets two players choose a dispel. A visible Pillar of Fire persists, scatters each turn, damages enemies it crosses, and can be dispelled. The Daemonsmith can re-roll one Artillery or Scatter die for the nearby Deathshrieker once per turn. Its Sorcerer’s Curse, Blackshard protection, ensorcelled weapon, Resolute and Stubborn rules are included. The Battlemage has Magical Attacks and Magic Resistance (-1). **House rule:** the published Daemonsmith lore list does not include Battle Magic; it is allowed here because this scenario requests it.

Click a regiment on the flock table to open orders beside it. Drag the popup by its **↔ DRAG UNIT ORDERS** heading, or focus that heading and use the arrow keys. During Movement, declare charges, roll them, then move remaining units. If no legal charge is available, the charge steps are skipped. Regiments that can declare a charge remain bright; those that cannot are gray. A declared charger gains a gold outline and a gold line to its target. A charge may use one measured approach wheel and then a free wheel to align after contact. Front, flank and rear facing is fixed when the charge is declared. Chaos Dwarfs move 3 inches or march 6; Orcs and State Troops move 4 or march 8. Marching near an enemy may need a Leadership test. Advance, wheel, or reform using the segmented inch bar. After a unit finishes its action, orders advance to the next eligible regiment on the right; a partial move keeps the current popup open. The dice pit is on the battlefield. Gray units have finished their phase actions.

During charge declarations, every defender chooses Hold or Flee, and an eligible missile regiment may instead Stand & Shoot. The chosen defender is marked on the table and its reaction popup opens automatically; charges cannot be rolled until all reactions are chosen. Flee pivots away from the charger and moves the sum of 2D6 inches immediately. A charger that reaches the fleeing target runs it down; otherwise it pursues for its full charge range. A missile defender must face the charger and start at least the charger's Movement value away to Stand & Shoot. The reaction fires before the charge roll, with its own −1 to hit, no long-range penalty, and the charger treated as entering weapon range. Blunderbusses ignore the Stand & Shoot penalty.

During Shooting, click a missile regiment. If no unit has a legal shot, Shooting is skipped automatically. Until the army shoots, fires a war machine or casts a spell, **← Back to Movement** reopens Remaining Moves with the turn's undo history (also from Combat when Shooting was skipped and no fight has started). Regiments in combat cannot be shot at. Its 90° front arc shades close range (the first half of the weapon's range) and far range on the table. Choose a highlighted target directly on the board or in the unit popup. The popup shows firing models, range band, movement/long-range modifiers, and the needed hit and wound rolls. Blunderbusses fire D3 shots per model and use Volley Fire from rear ranks; their weapon ignores the long-range penalty. Empire crossbows and Orc warbows fire from the front rank. Shots, wounds, armour saves, and casualties are rolled; an ordinary move gives −1 to hit. A unit that marched or is fleeing cannot shoot or Stand & Shoot. Hits show a brief impact flash on the target.

Click the Deathshrieker or its roster entry during the Chaos Dwarf Shooting phase. Choose **Demolition Rockets** (3″ blast, S3 with S6/AP −3 under the central hole) or **Infernal Incendiaries** (5″ blast, S3, Flaming Attacks). Both have a 12–48″ range. Select a target to place the measured circular template over its centre, then roll animated Artillery and Scatter dice. An arrow moves the template the Artillery distance; a Hit lands on the aim point. Indirect Fire is available when line of sight is blocked and a Hit still scatters by Artillery minus crew BS3. Models only partly under the template are hit on 4+. The impact circle stays on the battlefield while hits, wounds, saves, casualties, and Incendiary Panic are resolved. A Misfire uses the Black Powder Misfire table. The launcher uses its 50 × 75 mm base with three visible 25 mm crew bases. The aim point follows Bombardment and is fixed at the centre of the chosen unit. Scatter direction uses eight discrete directions in this digital prototype. The war machine is stationary and cannot be shot at, but enemy regiments can charge it (see War machines in combat below).

When Empire is selected, deploy both Great Cannons in the blue zone. During the Empire Shooting phase, click a cannon on the board. Choose **Cannonball** (60″, S10, AP −3) and an aim point 0–10″ short of a target, then roll strike and bounce Artillery dice. The ball can hit models along its path, up to one per rank or file of each regiment. Or choose **Grapeshot** (12″, S4, AP −1) for an Artillery die of automatic hits on the selected regiment. A Misfire uses the same Black Powder table as the launcher. Each cannon has a 50 × 75 mm base and three visible crew. Cannon and rocket impacts show a short burst at the landing point and on units hit. Cannons are stationary and cannot be shot at, but they can be charged.

During Combat, click either engaged regiment. First show each side's Initiative, then roll attacks one Initiative tier at a time. Compare combat result, roll the loser's Break test, and choose Shieldwall when available. Move the loser back, then the winner chooses follow up/pursuit or restraint. A pursuer that destroys a fleeing unit still moves its rolled distance. If attacks wipe out the enemy before the Break test, choose an overrun straight ahead or test Leadership to restrain and reform. An overrunner that does not contact a fresh enemy may test Leadership to reform afterward. Pursuit and overrun can contact a fresh enemy as a charge, then wheel to align flush with it; they fight in the next turn. Intervening units stop movement, and a battlefield edge stops a pursuit. An overrun (2D6 straight ahead, not Movement-based) that carries the unit off the battlefield removes it temporarily; it returns just inside that edge, facing inward and near its exit point, in its next Movement phase before Remaining Moves, and counts as having moved. An overrun into an enemy already in an unresolved combat is not simulated (one-on-one combats only), and no unit here has Swiftstride. Empty phases and Movement subphases advance automatically. Dead models stay dark; surviving fighting models are bright red, green or blue. A unit that breaks and survives can take a 2D6 Rally test in its next Strategy phase.

## War machines in combat

The Deathshrieker and the Great Cannons can be charged like any regiment, from any side, and may only Hold. The machine and its crew form a single model; the crew bases are tokens. In combat the model uses its crew profile (Chaos Dwarf crew WS3 S3 T4 I2 Ld9; Empire crew WS3 S3 T3 I3 Ld7) with one Attack per surviving crew token and no armour save. Each unsaved wound removes a crew token, and the last one destroys the machine. War machines have no flanks or rear, no ranks and no Shieldwall. A war machine never moves: if it breaks, the crew abandon it and it is destroyed; after Fall Back or Give Ground it stays in the combat. A winning war machine cannot follow up, pursue or overrun. An engaged war machine cannot fire. Crew armour is not modelled.

## Measurements and rules

- Table: 72 × 48 inches (6 × 4 feet). Deployment zones: 12 inches deep. The SVG scales the board to the available screen; measurements remain tabletop inches.
- Each regiment: 5 files × 4 ranks. Chaos Dwarf and State Troop bases are 25 × 25 mm; their blocks measure 125 × 100 mm. Orc bases are 30 × 30 mm; their blocks measure 150 × 120 mm.
- Renegades 2.0 Chaos Dwarf Warriors: M3 WS4 BS3 S3 T4 W1 I2 A1 Ld9, hand weapons, heavy armour and shields. Base armour 4+; Parry improves the shield save to 3+ in these close combats.
- Orc Mob (Orc Boy): M4 WS3 BS3 S3 T4 W1 I3 A1 Ld6, hand weapons and light armour (6+). A 3-inch charge gives Furious Charge; Choppas reroll 1s to wound and add Armour Piercing on a charge. Warband adds rank bonus to Leadership where permitted and automatically rerolls a failed charge range. An Orc Mob able to charge tests Impetuous; failure forces a charge.
- Empire State Troops (State Trooper): M4 WS3 BS3 S3 T3 W1 I3 A1 Ld7, hand weapons, light armour and the optional shield. Base armour 5+; Parry improves it to 4+ in combat.
- Blunderbuss Decimators: M3 WS3 BS3 S3 T4 W1 I2 A1 Ld9, heavy armour, 12-inch blunderbuss (S3, AP −1, Multiple Shots D3, Volley Fire). The 5 × 4 regiment is on 25 mm bases.
- Deathshrieker Rocket Launcher: T6 W3, 50 × 75 mm base, with Chaos Dwarf Crew (M3 WS3 BS3 S3 T4 W3 I2 A3 Ld9) shown on three adjacent 25 × 25 mm bases. Demolition Rockets use the 3″ template; Infernal Incendiaries use the 5″ template.
- Empire Great Cannons: two stationary T6 W3 machines on 50 × 75 mm bases, each with three visible crew. Cannonball reaches 60″ at S10/AP −3; grapeshot reaches 12″ at S4/AP −1.
- State Missile Troops: M4 WS3 BS3 S3 T3 W1 I3 A1 Ld7, crossbows (30 inches, S4). Their Sergeant has BS4. Orc Mob with warbows: M4 WS3 BS3 S3 T4 W1 I3 A1 Ld6, warbows (24 inches, S3), light armour and 30 mm bases.
- Fighting rank and Press of Battle models attack. Hit, wound and save targets use the opposing profiles. Charging gives an Initiative bonus. Combat result includes unsaved wounds, ranks, Close Order, Massed Infantry and flank/rear bonuses. A losing unit takes a 2D6 Break test with the combat-result difference.
- Full command: M–S–C occupy the middle three front-rank squares, with the standard at the centre. The Orc Boss has Leadership 7 and 2 Attacks; the State Missile Troop Sergeant has BS4 and 1 Attack. Other champions have 2 Attacks. A living standard adds +1 combat result. If otherwise tied, a sole musician adds +1 combat result. A living musician adds +1 Leadership to rally tests and march tests made within 8 inches of an enemy, up to Leadership 10. Ordinary casualties are removed before command models in this simplified one-on-one combat system.
- If applicable, the loser may choose Shieldwall to turn Fall Back in Good Order into Give Ground. Resolute reduces flee/pursuit distance by one inch. Rally uses 2D6 at or below Leadership 9; a rallied unit cannot charge that turn.

This is a small tabletop prototype. One-on-one engagements only. The charge planner supports one measured approach wheel plus a free alignment wheel, for front, flank and rear charges. Other terrain, character joining and challenges, magical command upgrades, victory conditions, multiple charges, charge redirection, and full compulsory fleeing movement are not simulated. Fleeing through intervening units and Panic caused by a nearby flee are not simulated. Shooting does not yet include panic from missile casualties, cover terrain, or unusual weapon options. Casualty squares stay in their original positions rather than closing ranks. Regiments keep their 5 × 4 footprint. The game resets on reload. Renegades is a community army list, separate from the official Games Workshop legacy list.

The post-combat choice order follows this prototype's requested play flow: the loser moves before the winner chooses. In the published Old World rules, the winner chooses whether to follow up or pursue before the loser moves. A fresh enemy contacted by pursuit fights in the next turn and counts the pursuer as charging. Pursuers currently stop at the battlefield edge instead of leaving and returning as reinforcements. The charge planner searches in 5-degree wheel steps, so some otherwise legal tabletop approaches may still need manual repositioning in an earlier turn.

## Files and checks

- `Play.html`: bundled, standalone game.
- `dist/`: editable HTML, CSS and JavaScript source for a local server.
- `dist/ai.mjs`: optional opponent decisions and turn sequencing.
- `package-standalone.mjs`: rebuilds `Play.html` from `dist/`.
- `test-*.mjs`: movement, charges, model eligibility, movement budget, combat, cannon, AI, and presentation checks. Run each with Node.js.
